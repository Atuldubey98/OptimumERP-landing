## 🚀 Overview

OptimumERP is a modern ERP built for the AI era. It provides complete financial management coupled with intelligent, privacy-first agents, offering a complete, uncompromising suite of tools designed to handle every aspect of your enterprise operations.

## ✨ Core Features

### 🤖 Powerful AI Automation
* **AI-Powered Workflows (OptiBot):** A natural language chat interface to automatically create bills, manage parties, and query complex financial data in seconds.
* **AI Document Auto-Fill:** Instantly extract structured, highly accurate data from PDF and image-based invoices or purchase orders, eliminating manual entry.

### 💼 Comprehensive Financials
* Full suite support for Invoices, Quotes, Proforma, and Purchase Orders with complete inventory tracking and ledger management.
* **Expense Management:** Track & categorize business costs.
* **Tax Management:** Multi-tax types (GST, VAT).
* **Multi-Currency:** Custom decimal and format configs.

### 📦 Inventory & Party Management
* **Parties & Vendors:** Comprehensive balance tracking and real-time financial transaction tracking via Party Ledgers.
* **Product Catalog:** Multi-unit support & categories.
* **Bulk Operations:** CSV import for parties & products.

### 🔄 Tally XML Sync
* Seamlessly import and synchronize your Masters and Vouchers with existing Tally software to keep your legacy systems up to date.

### 📊 Analytics & Compliance
* **Real-Time Dashboard:** Track invoices, expenses & parties.
* **GST Reports:** GSTR1 and GSTR2 compliance.
* **Transaction Reports:** Deep filtering & analysis.
* **Data Export:** Download as PDF or Excel.

### 🏢 Organization & Security
* **Multi-Org Support:** Manage multiple business units.
* **Role-Based Access:** Admin & staff privileges.
* **Authentication:** Email OTP & Google OAuth.
* **Financial Years:** Close & transition fiscal years.

## 🧠 Multi-Provider BYOK Architecture

Bring Your Own Key (BYOK) for cloud models, or run completely offline with local models. You are always in control of your data privacy.

* **Local Offline Processing:** Connect to local Ollama models for 100% air-gapped data privacy.
* **Groq Integration:** Lightning-fast inference via Groq for high-speed automated workflows.
* **Custom Integrations:** Additional providers can be integrated on request to fit your unique workflow.
* **Enterprise Security:** Role-based access control, secure session management, and automated network logging.

## ☁️ Deployment Flexibility

Choose the infrastructure that best aligns with your security compliance and operational requirements:

* **SaaS Deployment:** A modern, fully managed cloud deployment. Get up and running instantly with zero infrastructure overhead. Perfect for distributed teams.
* **On-Premise Installation:** Install directly on your client servers for ultimate local-first privacy. Ensures sensitive financial data never leaves your internal network.
